Our Journey
From a two-person engineering studio to an AI, cloud & automation partner for enterprise clients.
  • 2021

    Founded

    Phorge Developers launches as a software engineering studio in Hyderabad, taking on custom builds for early-stage companies.

  • 2022

    First Enterprise Engagement

    Delivered a cloud migration for a regional logistics company, cutting their infrastructure costs by 40%.

  • 2023

    Cloud Practice

    Launched a dedicated cloud transformation and cost-optimization practice as demand outgrew one-off projects.

  • 2024

    Phorge.AI

    Spun up the AI venture — automation, LLM-powered support, and multi-agent systems for enterprise workflows.

  • 2025

    Cross-Border Growth

    Opened engagements across Southeast Asia and the Gulf, and grew the team past 60 people.

  • 2026

    Today

    A team of 80+ shipping AI, cloud, and automation for ambitious businesses — and still hiring.

Our Values
Applying these is what helps us move fast, keep clients' trust, and enjoy the work.

Ownership

We treat every engagement like our own business. No hand-holding, no hiding behind process.

Radical Simplicity

We cut complexity wherever we find it — in code, in workflows, and in how we talk to clients.

Transparency

We share context early, flag risk before it becomes a problem, and never dress up a bad number.

Craftsmanship

We'd rather ship something excellent a week late than something mediocre on time.

Velocity

We move like a startup even on enterprise engagements — bias to action, tight feedback loops.

Inclusion by Design

Different backgrounds build better systems. We hire for range, not sameness.

Trust

Every teammate owns a piece of what we're building, and we trust each other to act in its interest.

Curiosity

We ask more questions than we answer, especially about the problem before we start solving it.

How We Work
Enjoy the job, and love your life outside of it.

Remote-First, India-Anchored

Our core team works from Hyderabad, with specialists distributed across time zones for global client coverage.

Build What You Ship

Engineers sit close to client outcomes — you'll see your automation running in production, not just in a sprint demo.

One All-Hands, Real Autonomy

A single weekly sync keeps everyone aligned. Everything else is async, documented, and owned end-to-end.

Learning Stipend

Every team member gets an annual budget for courses, certifications, and conference travel.

Flat, Low-Ego Structure

Titles don't gate opinions. The best idea wins the room, regardless of who's speaking.

Fridays for R&D

One day a week is protected for internal tools, exploration, and the ideas nobody assigned you.
